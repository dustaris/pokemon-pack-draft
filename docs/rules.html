2. Eligibility

  • You must be 18 or older and a legal resident of the United States at the time you join.
  • You must have a free WD Card Shop customer account and confirm eligibility in the game ("Join rewards").
  • One account per person. Creating or using more than one account to take part, or sharing an account, disqualifies all accounts involved.
  • Employees of Sponsor and their immediate household members are not eligible.

3. How to take part

Play Pokédex Pack Draft at wdcardshop.com/pages/pokedex-pack-drafts while signed in to your WD Card Shop account, and join rewards in the game. The game and its packs are free; nothing needs to be bought. Each time you open a pack while you have a prize chance available, that pack is entered into a random prize draw, and any prize is shown when the pack opens.

  • Daily chances: you get 3 prize chances per day (Pacific Time). Unused chances do not carry over to the next day.
  • Email bonus: subscribing to the WD Card Shop email list adds 3 prize chances, one time per account. You can also get the same 3 bonus chances without subscribing by emailing support@wdcardshop.com with the subject "Pack Draft free entry" from your account email (one time per person).
  • Packs opened when you have no prize chances left, or before you join rewards, are not entered.

4. Prizes and odds

Each entered pack is drawn once, with the following odds of winning each prize:

PrizeOdds per entered packApproximate retail value

A pack can win at most one prize. Overall odds of winning any prize are about 1 in 20 per entered pack.

Monthly limit. Store credit prizes are limited to $150 in total across all players per calendar month (Pacific Time). Once that amount is reached, store credit prizes can't be won for the rest of the month, and packs can still win discount codes. The number of discount codes is not limited.

Store credit is added to the winner's WD Card Shop customer account, applies at checkout on wdcardshop.com, and expires 90 days after it is issued.

Discount codes take 5% off the order subtotal, up to $50 off; work once; work only for the winning customer account; need an order subtotal of at least $25 before shipping and taxes; cannot be combined with other discount codes; and expire 30 days after they are issued.

Prizes have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be transferred or sold.

5. How winners are chosen

Winners are chosen at random by Sponsor's server when an entered pack opens. What you do in the game (which pack you open, which card you keep) does not change the odds. Your number of entries depends only on your prize chances as described in section 3.

6. Verification, fair play and disqualification

  • Store credit won by an account created less than 7 days earlier, or by an account flagged by our automated checks, is reviewed before it is issued. The prize shows as "being verified" in the game until then, usually within a few days. Sponsor may ask a winner to confirm eligibility.
  • Sponsor uses automated checks to detect edited game saves, scripted play and other tampering.
  • Sponsor may withhold or cancel prizes for cheating, using bots, scripts or modified game data, using more than one account, exploiting bugs, or otherwise acting unfairly. Sponsor's decisions are final.
  • Prizes are delivered on the winner's account and listed under "Your prizes" in the game. If a winner cannot be verified, the prize is not awarded.
