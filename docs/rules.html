2. Eligibility

  • You must be 18 or older and a legal resident of the United States at the time you join.

3. How to take part

Play Pokédex Pack Draft at wdcardshop.com while signed in. The game is free; nothing needs to be bought. Rewards come from two activities:

  • Monthly leaderboard. Each day you play the ranked Daily Puzzle, your best score (0–100) counts. Every player gets the same Pokémon and puzzle; finding the perfect order scores 100 on the first try, 90 on the second and 80 on the third, and other orders score their percentage of the best possible result, minus 10 per extra try. Your monthly score is the total of your daily best scores from 12:00 a.m. on the first day of a calendar month to 11:59 p.m. on its last day, Pacific Time. You get up to 3 ranked tries per day. Ranked battles are scored on Sponsor's server. Solving the puzzle using software, scripts or other accounts is not allowed.
  • Journey milestone. Beating all 9 region Champions in the game.

5. Ties, odds and how winners are chosen

Monthly ties are broken by the most Daily Puzzle stars that month, then by the earliest time the tied score was reached. Monthly winners are determined after the month ends; codes are issued after review, usually within a week. Game results include random elements such as pack contents and battle outcomes, so the chance of winning depends on the number of players and how each player performs.

  • Sponsor uses automated checks to detect edited game saves, impossible progress, and other tampering, and may re-play battles on its servers to confirm results.
  • Sponsor may withhold or cancel rewards and remove players from leaderboards for cheating, using bots, scripts or modified game data, using more than one account, exploiting bugs, or otherwise acting unfairly. Sponsor's decisions are final.
  • Codes are delivered in the game on the winner's account. If a winner cannot be verified, the reward may go to the next eligible player or not be awarded.
