2. Eligibility

  • You must be 18 or older and a legal resident of the United States at the time you join.
  • You must have a free WD Card Shop customer account and confirm eligibility in the game ("Join rewards").
  • One account per person. Creating or using more than one account to take part, or sharing an account, disqualifies all accounts involved.
  • Employees of Sponsor and their immediate household members are not eligible.

3. How to take part

Play Pokédex Pack Draft at wdcardshop.com/pages/pokedex-pack-drafts while signed in. The game is free; nothing needs to be bought. You get free packs every day, keep one card from each pack, and build a collection. Rewards come from:

  • Secret bonus. A hidden reward for a collection milestone, revealed in the game when you reach it (details in section 4).
  • Monthly collectors. Your collection score goes up as you add Pokémon (Common 1 point, Uncommon 2, Rare 5, Legendary 20), upgrade card grades (each grade adds the card's points again) and collect Shinies (+10 each). The monthly leaderboard ranks the points each player gained during a calendar month, Pacific Time.
  • Showcase of the Month. Signed-in players can publish a showcase of up to 6 cards to the public gallery. Each month Sponsor may pick one showcase it likes best.

5. Ties, odds and how winners are chosen

Pack contents are random, so the chance of finding Shinies or topping the monthly board depends on luck, how often you play and how many people play. Monthly ties are broken by the earliest time the tied total was reached. Monthly winners are determined after the month ends; codes are issued after review, usually within a week. Showcase of the Month is chosen at Sponsor's discretion, and Sponsor may choose not to pick one in a given month.

6. Verification, fair play and disqualification

  • All monthly and Showcase of the Month winners, and any secret bonus reward flagged by our automated checks or earned by an account less than 7 days old, are reviewed before a code is issued. Sponsor may ask a potential winner to confirm eligibility.
  • Sponsor uses automated checks to detect edited game saves, impossible progress (such as more cards than packs opened) and other tampering.
  • Sponsor may withhold or cancel rewards, remove showcases and remove players from leaderboards for cheating, using bots, scripts or modified game data, using more than one account, exploiting bugs, inappropriate names, or otherwise acting unfairly. Sponsor's decisions are final.
  • Codes are delivered in the game on the winner's account. If a winner cannot be verified, the reward may go to the next eligible player or not be awarded.
